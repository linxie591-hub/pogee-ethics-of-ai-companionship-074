Ethics of AI Companionship

Building a companion that people care about is an ethical responsibility.

The more real a character feels, the more the design choices matter: transparency about what the AI is, healthy boundaries, and honest handling of emotional dependence.

Getting this right is not a constraint on the product - it is what earns long-term trust.

Care is a feature, and it has to be designed in from the start.
